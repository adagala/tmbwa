# TMBWA Member User Guide

This guide explains how members can pay contributions, add funds to their account, review their financial history, download receipts and statements, and view notifications.

## Before you begin

- Sign in with your TMBWA email address and password.
- Use a current web browser and allow pop-up windows when printing a receipt or statement.
- Members can view only their own profile and financial records.
- Amounts are shown in Kenya shillings (KES), and transaction times use East Africa Time.

## Pay a contribution using M-Pesa STK Push

1. Sign in to TMBWA.
2. Select **Profile** from the navigation menu.
3. Scroll to **Contribution History**.
4. Select the contribution month you want to pay.
5. The contribution details open in a side panel. Review the amount, current status, **Balance due**, and the **History** of payments and corrections for that month.
6. Check **M-Pesa phone number**. It starts as the phone number registered to your membership; change it to send the prompt to another phone, for example a family member paying on your behalf.
7. Select **Pay KES …**. The button shows the balance currently due.
8. Enter the M-Pesa PIN on that phone and confirm the payment.
9. Wait for the payment to be processed and reconciled. A successful M-Pesa prompt does not by itself mean that the contribution balance has already been updated.

The STK button is shown only when your membership is active and the selected contribution has an outstanding balance. The request is for the full balance currently due on that contribution.

Do not repeatedly request another STK Push while a payment is still being processed. If the prompt expires, is cancelled, or does not arrive, confirm in M-Pesa that no payment was made before trying again or contacting an administrator.

## Add funds to your account

You can pay any amount into your account in advance. TMBWA uses it to pay your contributions automatically.

1. Sign in to TMBWA and select **Profile**.
2. Next to **Account balance**, select **Add funds**.
3. Enter the amount in whole shillings. The dialog shows how the amount will be used before you confirm.
4. Check **M-Pesa phone number**. It starts as the phone number registered to your membership; change it to send the prompt to another phone.
5. Select **Send M-Pesa prompt**, then approve the prompt with the M-Pesa PIN on that phone.
6. Keep the dialog open to see when the top-up is received, or check **Account balance** later.

After you send the prompt, the dialog shows one of these messages:

- **Waiting for M-Pesa** — approve the prompt on the phone you entered.
- **Top-up received** — your account has been credited and any unpaid contributions have been paid.
- **Payment received** — M-Pesa accepted the payment and an administrator will confirm it and credit your account shortly. Do not pay again.
- **Payment not yet confirmed** — TMBWA could not confirm the outcome. Wait for an administrator to verify the payment before trying again.
- **Top-up not completed** — the prompt was cancelled, expired, or failed. If M-Pesa deducted money, contact an administrator; otherwise you can try again.

How a top-up is applied:

- It first pays any unpaid or partly paid contributions, oldest month first.
- Anything left stays on your account and pays each new monthly contribution automatically when it is created.
- There is no maximum amount. Credit cannot currently be refunded or withdrawn, and it stays on your account if your membership becomes inactive.

**Add funds** is available only while your membership is active. You cannot start a top-up while a contribution STK payment is still in progress, or start a contribution payment while a top-up is in progress.

## View contribution history

1. Select **Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Contribution History**.
3. Review the monthly list. Each entry shows the amount collected and one of these statuses:
   - **Paid** — the contribution has no remaining balance.
   - **Partial** — a payment has been allocated, but a balance remains.
   - **Unpaid** — no payment has been allocated and the full amount remains due.
4. Select a month to view its total amount, balance due, payment references, allocated amounts, and payment dates.

If any month since you joined has no contribution recorded, a note above the list shows those months. You aren't charged for them until an administrator adds them. Contact an administrator if you have questions about a listed month.

## View transactions and download a receipt

1. Select **Profile**.
2. In the financial history section, select the **Transactions** tab.
3. Find the required transaction by date, type, reference, or amount.
4. Select the underlined receipt number.
5. In the browser print window, choose **Save as PDF** to download the receipt, or choose a printer to print it.

The receipt includes your name and member number, receipt number, payment date, reference, payment type, total amount, contribution allocation, and any unallocated account credit.

If selecting a receipt does nothing, allow pop-ups for the TMBWA site and try again. On a narrow phone screen, the receipt-number action may not be displayed; use a wider screen or desktop browser.

## Download or print a member statement

1. Select **Profile**.
2. Under **Settings**, optionally choose **Statement from** and **To** dates.
3. Select **Print statement**.
4. In the browser print window, choose **Save as PDF** to download the statement, or print it.

Leaving either date blank includes all available records on that side of the period. The statement shows charges, payments, references, your account balance, and outstanding contributions.

## View notifications

1. Select **Notifications** from the navigation menu.
2. Review **Your inbox**. Unread items have a red indicator.
3. Select **Mark as read** on an individual notification, or **Mark all as read**.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** when the inbox contains more than one page.

Notifications may include payment, contribution, reversal, and arrears updates. Payment confirmations appear after reconciliation.

To change the preference, use **Turn off** or **Turn on** under **In-app notifications**. Changing this preference does not change any contribution or payment record.

## Getting help

When reporting a problem, provide the affected contribution month, receipt or reference number, approximate time, and the message shown on screen. Never send your M-Pesa PIN, password, authentication code, or another secret.

For payment problems, first check your M-Pesa transaction history. If M-Pesa shows a completed payment but TMBWA has not updated, give the receipt number to an administrator for reconciliation. Do not initiate another payment until the first payment has been checked.
